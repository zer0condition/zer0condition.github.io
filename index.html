05
The long road down
From neighbourhood PC repairs to stealth hypervisors.
2015 / age 9
First build
Assembled my first PC from individual components at nine years old. Software stopped feeling like magic and started feeling like machinery.
2015 — 2019
OS swaps, partitions, repairs
Rotated through Windows XP / 7 / 8 weekly. Fixed neighbours' and my father's office machines as the cheapest way to earn hands-on hours.
2020 / lockdown
The rabbit hole
Picked up C, C++ and x86 assembly. Self-taught virtual memory, paging, and how the kernel actually talks to the processor.
2021 — 2023
Kernel
Built custom tooling and reverse-engineered anti-cheats, AVs, EDRs and XDRs to understand where their seams are.
2024 — now
Hypervisors & new tooling
Shifted to hypervisor research. Shipped Ophion (stealth VT-x), BusterCall (HVCI bypass via donor PFNs), xSIMD (cross-platform SIMD assembler), and NTMemory (usermode NT explorer).
