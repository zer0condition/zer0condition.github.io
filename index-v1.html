04 — Journey

The long road down

From swapping Windows installations for fun to writing stealth hypervisors.

2015 · Beginning

First build at nine

Obsessed with computers from childhood. Assembled my first PC from individual components at nine years old — the moment software stopped feeling like magic and started feeling like machinery.

2015–2019 · Tinkering

OS installs, partitions, neighbourhood repairs

Rotated through Windows XP, 7, and 8 weekly for fun. Fixed neighbours' and my father's office machines when they broke — the cheapest way to earn hands-on hours.

2020 · The rabbit hole

Low-level languages during lockdown

Pandemic lockdown. Picked up C, C++ and x86 assembly. Self-taught virtual memory, paging, and how the kernel actually talks to the processor.

2021–2023 · Kernel

Windows kernel development

Deep dive into kernel internals. Built custom tooling and reverse-engineered the internals of well-known anti-cheats, AVs, EDRs and XDRs to understand where their seams are.

2024–2026 · Hypervisors & CPUs

Virtualization, CPU security modules, and new tooling

Shifted toward hypervisor research and precise CPU-level control. Shipped Ophion (stealth VT-x HV), BusterCall (HVCI bypass via donor PFN swaps), xSIMD (x86/ARM/RISC-V SIMD assembler), and NTMemory (usermode NT explorer).

Also picked up digital music production as a parallel creative outlet.
